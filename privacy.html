Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

reel is a personal tool used only by its author, Juan Cerquera, on his own computer.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, reel uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's videos and analytics (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly).

How data is stored and used

The sign-in token and the analytics numbers are stored only on the owner's computer. They are used only to publish the owner's videos and measure how they perform. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising, and reel has no servers that collect user data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions. Deleting the local token file removes the stored data.

Third-party terms

By using reel's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
